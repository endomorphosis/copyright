# Pre-1976 US Copyright Statutes

This directory contains the text of US copyright law as it existed between the Copyright Act of 1790 and the Copyright Act of 1976, which rewrote Title 17 into its modern form.

For English copyright law *before* the Copyright Act of 1790 — including the Statute of Anne (1710) and its predecessors — see the `pre-1790/` directory (present in earlier commits of this repository).

Before 1976, the structure of US copyright law changed significantly with each major revision:

- **1790–1830**: The original Copyright Act, a short standalone statute
- **1831–1869**: Expanded by the Act of 1831
- **1870–1908**: Consolidated into the Revised Statutes (Title 60, later Title 17)
- **1909–1975**: The Copyright Act of 1909, which governed for 67 years

Each file represents the full text of the copyright statute as it read after a given act's amendments. When the 1976 Act takes effect (January 1, 1978), this directory is removed and replaced by the `sections/` directory with the modern Title 17 structure.
