Editorial Notes
References in Text

Pub. L. 108–419,
which is 6 months after Nov. 30, 2004, subject to transition provisions, see section 6 of
Pub. L. 108–419,

The enactment of the Copyright Royalty and Distribution Reform Act of 2004 and such date of enactment, referred to in subsec. (b)(6)(A), (B), probably mean the date of enactment of
Pub. L. 108–419,

Pub. L. 103–69,
which was formerly set out as a note under section 60–1 of Title 2, The Congress.
