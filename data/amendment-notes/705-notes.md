# Amendment Notes for 17 U.S.C. § 705

Editorial Notes
Amendments
2000-Subsec. (a).
Pub. L. 106–379
amended subsec. (a) generally. Prior to amendment, subsec. (a) read as follows: "The Register of Copyrights shall provide and keep in the Copyright Office records of all deposits, registrations, recordations, and other actions taken under this title, and shall prepare indexes of all such records."
