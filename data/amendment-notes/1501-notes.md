# Amendment Notes for 17 U.S.C. § 1501

Statutory Notes and Related Subsidiaries
Severability
Pub. L. 116–260,
div. Q, title II, §212(f), Dec. 27, 2020, 134 Stat. 2200
, provided that: "If any provision of this section [see Short Title of 2020 Amendment note set out under section 101 of this title], an amendment made by this section, or the application of such provision or amendment to any person or circumstance is held to be unconstitutional, the remainder of this section and the amendments made by this section, and the application of the provision or the amendment to any other person or circumstance, shall not be affected."
