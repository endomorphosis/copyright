# Prints and Labels Transfer Act of 1939

*An Act to transfer the registration of claims of copyright in prints and labels for articles of merchandise to the Copyright Office from the Patent Office. 53 Stat. 1142, Ch. 396 (July 31, 1939)*

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, That on and after the date on which this Act takes effect all claims of copyright in prints and labels for articles of merchandise shall be registered in the Copyright Office, in the Library of Congress, and the Register of Copyrights is hereby charged with the duty of registering such claims.

SEC. 2. That there shall be transferred to the Copyright Office, in the Library of Congress, all records of the Patent Office relating to the registration of claims of copyright in prints and labels, and all plates and copies deposited in connection with such claims.

SEC. 3. That with respect to claims of copyright in prints and labels registered in the Patent Office before the date on which this Act takes effect, the provisions of the copyright laws in force at the time of registration shall continue to govern, except that all administrative functions relating thereto shall be performed by the Register of Copyrights.

SEC. 4. That this Act shall take effect on July first, nineteen hundred and forty.

APPROVED, July 31, 1939.
