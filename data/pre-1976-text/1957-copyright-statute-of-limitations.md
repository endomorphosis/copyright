# Copyright Statute of Limitations Act of 1957

*An Act to amend Title 17, United States Code, entitled "Copyrights," with respect to the statute of limitations. Public Law 85-313, 71 Stat. 633 (September 7, 1957)*

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, That section 115 of title 17, United States Code, is amended to read as follows:

"SEC. 115. (a) CRIMINAL PROCEEDINGS.--No criminal proceeding shall be maintained under the provisions of this title unless the same is commenced within three years after the cause of action arose.

"(b) CIVIL ACTIONS.--No civil action shall be maintained under the provisions of this title unless the same is commenced within three years after the claim accrued."

APPROVED, September 7, 1957.
