# Copyright Law Codification Act of 1947

*An Act to codify and enact into positive law, Title 17 of the United States Code, entitled "Copyrights". 61 Stat. 652, Ch. 391 (July 30, 1947)*

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, That Title 17 of the United States Code, entitled "Copyrights," is codified and enacted into positive law and may be cited as "17 U. S. C., Sec. ---." The sections of Title 17 as set forth in section 2 of this Act shall be legal evidence of the law contained therein, in all the courts of the United States and of the several States and Territories.

SEC. 2. Title 17 of the United States Code is hereby enacted as follows:

SEC. 3. Any rights or liabilities now existing under previous Acts or portions thereof shall not be affected by the repeal of such Acts or portions thereof made by section two of this Act.

SEC. 4. If any part of Title 17, as set forth in section two of this Act, shall be held invalid, the remainder shall not be affected thereby.

SEC. 5. This Act shall take effect on September 1, 1947.

APPROVED, July 30, 1947.
