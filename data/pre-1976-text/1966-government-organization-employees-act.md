# Government Organization and Employees Act of 1966 (Copyright Provision)

*Pub. L. 89-554, 80 Stat. 378 (Sept. 6, 1966)*

AN ACT To enact title 5, United States Code, "Government Organization and Employees," codifying the general and permanent laws relating to the organization of the Government of the United States and to its civilian officers and employees.

*Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,* That the general and permanent laws relating to the organization of the Government of the United States and to its civilian officers and employees, generally, are revised, codified, and enacted as title 5 of the United States Code, entitled "Government Organization and Employees."

[Note: Only the copyright-related provision is reproduced below. The full act is a comprehensive recodification of Title 5, spanning 80 Stat. 378-632.]

## Copyright-Related Provision

### Section 7, Repealing Schedule (80 Stat. 631)

The following statutes and parts of statutes are repealed, subject to section 8:

[Among the statutes repealed:]

The Act of June 11, 1946, ch. 324, 60 Stat. 237 (the Administrative Procedure Act), the provisions of which were reenacted as subchapter II of chapter 5 and chapter 7 of the new title 5.

### Effect on Copyright Law

This repealing and reenactment required conforming changes in section 201 of title 17 (the 1909 Copyright Act as codified in 1947), which referenced the Administrative Procedure Act. After the enactment of Public Law 89-554, the cross-reference in section 201 of title 17 to the Administrative Procedure Act of June 11, 1946 (ch. 324, 60 Stat. 237) was understood to refer to the corresponding provisions as reenacted in title 5, United States Code, chapter 5, subchapter II, and chapter 7.

APPROVED, September 6, 1966.
