# An Act making Appropriations for the Legislative, Executive, and Judicial Expenses of the Government for the fiscal year ending June thirtieth, eighteen hundred and ninety-eight, and for other purposes.

*29 Stat. 544, Ch. 265 (February 19, 1897)*

[Copyright-relevant provision:]

That the Librarian of Congress shall appoint a Register of Copyrights, at a salary of four thousand dollars per annum, who, under the direction and supervision of the Librarian of Congress, shall perform all the duties relating to copyrights.

APPROVED, February 19, 1897.
