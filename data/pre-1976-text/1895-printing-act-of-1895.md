# Printing Act of 1895 (Copyright Provisions)

*Act of January 12, 1895, 53d Cong., 3d Sess., 28 Stat. 601, Ch. 23*

[Copyright-related provision extracted from the Printing Law of 1895]

SEC. 52. That no Government publication shall be copyrighted.

SEC. 61. That the Public Printer shall print and bind, for the use of the Government or of individual Members of Congress, and for distribution, only such publications as shall be authorized by law or which are necessary in the transaction of the public business required by law of the several departments and offices of the Government.

APPROVED, January 12, 1895.
