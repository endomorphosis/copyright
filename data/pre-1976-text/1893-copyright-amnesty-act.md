# Copyright Amnesty Act of 1893

*Act of March 3, 1893, 52d Cong., 2d Sess., 27 Stat. 743*

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled, That copies of copyrighted works deposited before March first, eighteen hundred and ninety-three, with the Librarian of Congress, in compliance with the existing laws, shall not be subjected to forfeiture of copyright on account of the failure to deposit within the time heretofore prescribed by law.

APPROVED, March 3, 1893.
