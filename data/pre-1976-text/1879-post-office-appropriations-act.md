# Post Office Appropriations Act of 1879 (Copyright Mailing Provision)

*Act of March 3, 1879, 45th Cong., 3d Sess., 20 Stat. 355, Ch. 180*

[Copyright-related provision extracted from the Post Office Appropriations Act]

Nothing in this act shall be construed as to allow the transmission through the mails of any publication which violates any copyright granted by the United States.

APPROVED, March 3, 1879.
